When your brain is running Too Many Tabs at once and your system is getting overwhelmed
Sometimes you need to slow down, turn it off and on again. 
Essentially, this is a website for people who struggle with anxiety/ anxiety attacks/ panic attacks/ general stress/ people who need a break, 
to reconnect and close some of the tabs to be less overwhelmed💖💞 
(also there are a lot of computer metaphors because sometimes it's easier to compare your feelings to something else to better understand)
